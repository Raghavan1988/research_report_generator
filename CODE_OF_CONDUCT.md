# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
